About

I am a Senior Research Fellow at the University of Otago, New Zealand, with an interdisciplinary background spanning epidemiology, economics, data science, and artificial intelligence.

My research focuses on environmental, spatial, and cancer epidemiology, infectious diseases, climate change and health, water quality, health equity, and the application of machine learning and explainable AI to population health and clinical research.

Research Interests

  • Climate change and health
  • Water quality and infectious diseases
  • Machine learning and explainable artificial intelligence
  • Health equity and population health
  • Environmental and health economics

Current Research

My current research includes the development of machine-learning approaches for predicting microbial contamination and associated health outcomes in drinking-water systems, investigation of environmental determinants of enteric infectious diseases, and AI-enabled approaches to health equity and clinical decision analytics.

I am also interested in cancer epidemiology and the development of explainable machine-learning and survival-analysis approaches for individualised risk stratification and prognostic modelling.
